The problem · why it's needed

Every LP position is two trades stapled together.

FEE-T side

Fee income — steady, grows with volume. The part everyone actually wants.

IL-T side

Impermanent loss — the bill you pay when price moves. The part nobody asked for.

Sold as one bundle. A treasury that wants yield is forced to take price risk; a trader who wants the vol bet is forced to market-make. IL is a tax you can't opt out of.

06 / 10
How — the engine

This can't exist without Reactive.

IL is a function of price. Price only changes on swaps. So the mark must update on every swap — without a bot polling on a timer.

Hook alone

Acts only during a swap — inline √-math would tax every trade.

Keeper / cron

Polls on a timer, burns gas regardless, and you must trust it.

Reactive SC

Fires on the swap event itself, computes cheaply in the ReactVM, calls back the mark.

07 / 10
What makes us different

Everyone else reduces IL. We separate it.

vs IL-protection hooks

They soften IL but the LP still holds it. We let the LP sell it.

vs options-on-LP

Those write derivatives next to a position. IL-T is a slice of the position.

vs insurance hooks

Those need a capital pool. We need only a counterparty + an upfront premium.

Prior art split the upside. We split the downside — the risk itself. Tranching a bond vs inventing the credit-default swap.
